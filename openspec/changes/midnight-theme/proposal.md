# Proposal

## Why

The planner works, but it looks like an unstyled default: the system font, one indigo accent, and every block drawn with the same weight of border. Nothing about it feels like a trip to Disneyland Paris.

The Plan also puts the day last. On a 390 × 844 phone, it opens on the trip's admin buttons, the start and end fields and a large ticket notice. The first timeline item starts about 700 px down, behind the fit status.

The goal is a look that feels magical but stays clean: Disney at night, in navy, silver and stars. It must stay readable on a phone in the sun, because that is where the app is used.

## What Changes

- **A night-sky theme across the app:**
  - A navy header with a quiet starfield and a serif wordmark. The Catalog and Plan tabs sit on navy, with a silver mark on the selected tab.
  - Content on cool silver-white surfaces, with navy text and silver hairlines, so lists and timelines stay readable outdoors.
  - Navy marks the primary actions, the selected day, the selected view and items in your plan.
  - Gold is kept for rating stars and a few small highlights.
  - Teal for "Fits", rose for "Over by" and late flags, and amber kept for warnings such as "Needs 120 cm".
  - Fonts: Fraunces for the wordmark and headings, Inter for everything else. Times use evenly spaced figures instead of the monospace font.
- **A constellation timeline:**
  - Each stop is a navy node with its number on a thin silver line that runs down the day.
  - Park changes and free time sit on that line.
  - Cards get lighter, with the time and name first, then area, walk and wait.
- **Small, purposeful motion:**
  - Sheets slide up, toasts fade in, a few header stars twinkle, and one small sparkle follows a successful "Optimize route".
  - None of it plays when the phone asks for reduced motion.
- **A compact Plan header**, on phones and wide screens:
  - **Trip row:** the trip picker as the title, then the number of days, Share, and a trip menu with New trip, Rename and Delete.
  - **Day hours:** one row with the start and end times, still editable in place and still labelled Start and End.
  - **Two-park reminder:** a compact note. It keeps the ticket reminder, the park order, "Switch order" and its hint.
  - **Toolbar:** the Timeline | Map switch and the route actions share one toolbar.
  - **Result:** on a 390 × 844 phone, the first timeline items are visible without scrolling.
- **The maps, recolored:**
  - A cool mist ground instead of green, area zones in colors that suit navy, and a navy route with gold-ringed stop numbers.
  - Shapes, the legend and behavior don't change.
- **The app's own mark and installed look:**
  - A new icon: a gold star over a silver calendar on navy, with no Disney logos, castle or characters.
  - A navy status bar and launch screen when installed.
- **No behavior changes.** Every action and every label the specs name stays. Saved trips and share links are untouched.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None. The theme and the Plan header belong to the app's frame.

### Modified Capabilities
- `app-shell`: adds requirements for the night-sky theme (including the app's own unofficial mark and fonts that work offline), readable contrast, reduced motion, and a Plan that shows the day near the top on phones, with trip actions in a trip menu.

## Impact

- **Styles:**
  - `src/index.css` gains the theme: named colors, fonts, the starfield's twinkle and the motion rules.
  - Tailwind's default palette is switched off, so leftover `slate-*` or `indigo-*` classes stop working and get caught.
- **UI:**
  - The shared primitives in `ui.tsx` are restyled.
  - Every component moves from raw Tailwind colors to the theme's names.
  - New: a header sky, a trip menu, and a compact day hours row.
  - Reworked: the timeline cards and rail, the Plan header, the fit bar, toasts and sheets.
  - The maps take their colors from the theme.
- **Dependencies:** `@fontsource-variable/inter` and `@fontsource-variable/fraunces` (OFL, self-hosted). No new runtime libraries.
- **App install:**
  - New `theme_color` and `background_color`, and a matching `theme-color` meta tag.
  - A new `icon.svg` and `favicon.svg`, with the PNG icons regenerated by `scripts/make-icons.ts`.
  - The fonts are precached for offline use.
- **Tests:**
  - Two catalog assertions on `bg-emerald-50` move to `data-planned`. An e2e selector on `span.block.font-medium` moves to a test id.
  - New tests: palette contrast, a guard against raw Tailwind colors, the phone fold check, the trip menu and reduced motion.
- **Data and store:** none.
- **Out of scope:**
  - A dark mode, or following the phone's dark setting. The named colors make it possible later.
  - Changes to the catalog's layout.
  - Replacing the browser's own confirm and rename prompts with themed sheets.
  - Illustrations and photos.
  - Any new feature.
