Each screen was compared with the Midnight mockup:

- The header is a single night-sky row.
- The phone has the bottom bar, with the fit strip above the tabs.
- My day shows the date chips, the night-sky day card, park sections whose stop numbers sit on one line, and the "Walk to …" pill at a park change.
- Sheets leave the header band free.
- Wide screens show Explore or Map beside My day.

Nothing is cut off or overlapping. A full-page shot draws the fixed bar halfway down the page, but that is how a full-page capture works.

Fixed during the review:

- A one-pixel seam of the page showed between rows of a park section, because the rows have fractional heights. Rows of one card now overlap by a pixel.
- One of the day card's stars sat on the Edit chevron. The band's stars now sit right of the date, below Edit.
- "Remove from day" wrapped onto two lines in a phone's stop sheet. Details now takes its own width, and Remove takes the rest.
- The row "Add" buttons in Explore were rounded squares. They are now circles, as in the mockup.
