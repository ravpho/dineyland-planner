To find out the detailed evacuation procedures for each attraction, please consult
the Accessibility Guides or speak with a Cast Member to assess the easiest and
safest way to be evacuated.
1
Easy evacuation conditions: short distance to cover, few steps, in open, well-lit
areas. The attraction’s evacuation exit is the standard exit.
2
Evacuation conditions include several dark, narrow passages and flights of
stairs to reach the attraction entrance. You are able to disembark from the
vehicle throughout the attraction journey.
3
Evacuation conditions may be complicated: several dark, narrow passages,
multiple flights of stairs to reach the attraction entrance. Disembarking from the
vehicle can involve a ladder, boat or platform.
